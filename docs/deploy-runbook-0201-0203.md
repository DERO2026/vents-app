## 6. The five pending withdrawals (A5)

Recorded 2026-10-30 (read-only), not re-queried for this runbook:
- 5 pending requests, ₦49,100 in total, oldest 2026-09-02 21:27 UTC;
- among them, two identical ₦20,000 requests made 10 seconds apart; the other three total ₦9,100;
- separately, 2 of the 5 earnings wallets disagree with their ledgers (₦32,150 and ₦1,000).

Which requests belong to which wallets was not recorded.

What 0203 requires for each one (enforced in SQL; proven locally):
1. Admin A requests `authorize_withdrawal` naming the request and the exact amount reviewed.
2. A **different super admin** approves it. Neither may be the organizer who asked for the money.
3. The organizer's bank account must still be active, with the recipient code captured at authorization. A changed or deactivated account blocks the claim until it is authorized again.
4. The claim then attaches the fixed reference `payout_<request id without dashes>`, so a retry can never send a second transfer.

Recommendation:
- **Before the window:** contact the organizer about the identical ₦20,000 pair. If it is a duplicate, reject one (a single admin can; it returns ₦20,000 to their available balance and moves no money out of VENTS). Do not pay anything on the old engine.
- **After the window, for each remaining request:**
  1. Run `admin_earnings_reconciliation()` and confirm the organizer's wallet has **no ledger gap**. If it is one of the two gap wallets, resolve the gap first with an audited adjustment; never pay out money the ledger cannot explain.
  2. Confirm the bank account with the organizer (the requests are about 2 months old).
  3. Then authorize with two admins and pay.

## 7. Rollback and recovery

| Row | Situation | Action |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | A migration fails while applying | It rolls back by itself (one transaction). Stop. Leave the migrations already applied in place: after 0201 only, nothing user-facing changes; after 0201+0202 the old frontend works and payouts are safer. Clear the kill switches. Investigate before another attempt; regenerate the migration if its drift guard refused |
| R2 | 0203 applied, frontend deploy fails | Keep purchases and payouts paused. Fix and redeploy. Do **not** reopen purchases on the old frontend (it shows 5% while 0203 charges 4%) |
| R3 | Defect found after deploy | **Prefer rolling forward** with a new migration. If a full rollback is unavoidable, do the steps in order: (1) pause purchases and payouts; (2) release every held amount to `balance_kobo` with a ledger and audit row per hold (`release_matured_earnings()` only releases matured holds, so anything not yet matured needs an approved, audited one-off); (3) restore the functions in one transaction: `SET vents.rollback_confirm = 'restore-pre-0201-0203';` then `supabase/rollback/pre_0201_0203_functions.sql`, plus `complete_organizer_payout` and `invite_recruiter_partnership` from the P2 output (the file refuses while anything is held, and without the setting); (4) Vercel instant rollback to deployment `dpl_8L1sqPf1u2FSggsC7DYYUzUqjAwa` (`5612ee0`), **only together with** step 3; (5) reconcile; (6) reopen |
| R4 | Data damage | Point-in-time restore to W2. Loses every purchase, payout and booking since; reconcile each against the Paystack dashboard before reopening |

Never drop `earnings_holds`, `earnings_recoveries`, `earn_profit_ledger`
or `earn_audit_log`. They are the record of what happened.

## 8. Risks still open

1. **`complete_organizer_payout` drift.** Production's body is not in the repository, and 0202 says it was written from the live definition. The 0202 guard refuses if that body changed after 2026-10-30, but nobody can diff 0202's rewrite against the live body without reading production. Owner or reviewer: compare P2's definition with 0202's before W5.
2. **`invite_recruiter_partnership`** has no recorded production md5. 0201 replaces it fully. Its rollback comes only from P2.
3. **Two-admin capacity.** If fewer than two eligible super admins exist (A4), no withdrawal can be paid after 0203.
4. **Ledger gaps** in 2 wallets. Paying a withdrawal from a gap wallet could pay out unexplained money (section 6).
5. **The window couples a database change with a frontend release.** There is no gradual rollout.
6. **Production facts are from 2026-10-30.** P1–P4 re-check them; anything else in this runbook that depends on them is marked.
