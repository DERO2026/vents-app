Help & Support

Need help with your VENTS account, a ticket, a payment, or something not working right? We're here to help — reach us directly, or check the common topics below.

Account access

I can't log in or I forgot my password

Use "Forgot Password" on the sign-in screen to reset your password by email. If you no longer have access to that email, contact support@getvents.com with the username or email on your account so we can verify and help you regain access.

I want to delete my account

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This anonymizes your data immediately and cannot be reversed. If you run into trouble, email support@getvents.com.

Tickets

How do I buy a ticket?

Open any event in the app, choose a ticket type and quantity, then tap "Buy Ticket." You can pay by card, bank transfer, or USSD. Your ticket and QR code appear under My Tickets right after payment.

I paid but my ticket isn't showing

Pull down to refresh on the My Tickets screen. If it still hasn't appeared after a few minutes, email support@getvents.com with your payment reference and we'll sort it out.

Payments

What payment methods are accepted?

Debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Verve), bank transfer, and USSD, all processed through Paystack — VENTS never stores your card details.

Can I get a refund?

Ticket refunds are handled by the event organizer — contact them directly from the event page. If an event was cancelled by its organizer, email support@getvents.com and we'll help.

Technical problems

Something in the app isn't working

Try closing and reopening the app, or updating to the latest version from the App Store/Play Store. If the problem continues, email support@getvents.com with a description of what happened and, if possible, a screenshot — we read every message.

Report a problem with an event or user

In the app, tap the (⋯) menu on the event or profile and choose "Report." For anything urgent, email support@getvents.com or message us on WhatsApp above.
